About

Belle Doron and Sebastiaan Dutilh compose original music for film, dance and theatre. They write to picture, to movement and to a director’s intent.

Much of their recent work has been for the stage. In 2023 they scored Blind to the Depths, a contemporary dance piece by Paxton Ricketts and Madoka Kariya that premiered at Sadler’s Wells in London. For The Witch’s Mark (2025), a multidisciplinary performance by Belle and The Beats, they co-wrote and produced the album and play the score live, responding to three dancers on stage. That way of working, with music and movement reacting to each other, goes back to RE:HUMANIZE (2020), an immersive dance show choreographed by Guido Dutilh.

For the screen they scored the international release of De Wilde Stad (2020), a feature-length nature documentary, and Stay (2020), a short film by Youp Kruijsdijk. In 2024 they wrote the music for the 1/OFF show that opened Amsterdam Fashion Week.

All of this grew out of CUT_, the electronic duo they started in 2014. Twelve years of writing, releasing and touring taught them how music carries emotion on screen, and their own songs have found their way there too. “Out of Touch” was used in a key scene of Netflix’s Élite, went to #1 on the global Shazam chart and has passed 80 million streams. Other tracks appear in Anne+, The L Word and This Is Gonna Be Great.

They also bring ten years of writing for commercials and sonic branding, for clients like Volkswagen, T-Mobile and Hunkemöller. That work taught them to move fast, stick to a brief and serve someone else’s story.
