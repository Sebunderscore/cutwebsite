About

CUT_ is Belle Doron and Sebastiaan Dutilh, an electronic duo from Amsterdam.

In 2019, after their debut album, the single “Out of Touch” was used in a key scene between Ander and Omar in Netflix’s Élite. It went to #1 on the global Shazam chart, stayed there for weeks and landed on viral playlists in more than ten countries, especially in the Spanish-speaking world. It has now passed 80 million streams.

Live, CUT_ plays as a four-piece with Jelle Huiberts on drums and Korné ter Steege on bass. In 2024 they toured Europe with The Ritual, including Sziget and Colors of Ostrava. The show adds touch, smell and taste to sound and light, so that people with a disability can take part as fully as anyone else. It is built around a ritual: a safe space where the audience is invited to play, move around and connect with strangers they would otherwise never have met.

Their latest album FEMINONE (2026) is more playful and upbeat than what came before, without losing its weight. The songs look for the humour in life’s darker corners and are written from a feminine perspective. In Belle’s words: “As a woman you deal with abuse and suppression regularly in your life, but womanhood also comes with sisterhood, desire and ever-changing power dynamics.”

Belle and Sebastiaan also write music for film, dance and theatre.
